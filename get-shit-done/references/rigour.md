# Rigour (`standard` | `spike`), risk-domain add-ons and the phase budget

A phase has one rigour level: `standard` (default, no ROADMAP line) or `spike`. `resolveRigour()` in `bin/rigour.js` resolves it; `init plan-phase` and `init execute-phase` emit `rigour`, `rigour_source` (`roadmap` | `default` | `rejected`), `spike_roots`, `rigour_rejected_reason`. There is no third level: depth for risky work is the add-ons column below. The init output also carries `rigour_notice` (a visible mismatch or unknown value, never authority).

```
### Phase 2: Short Name
**Rigour**: spike (OG2)
**Spike roots**: spike/, scripts/probes/
**Risk domains:** auth, uploads
```

**Decided once, printed always.** The newest APPROVED roadmap owner gate of the milestone is the single source of truth for spike phases and roots: `gsd-tools owner-gate request --kind roadmap --spike-phases N,M --spike-roots a,b`. new-milestone proposes rigour in the owner-gate summaries (OG1: prototype or `standard`; OG2: per phase the rigour, risk domains and budget). The ROADMAP `**Rigour**:` / `**Spike roots**:` lines are informational (the roadmapper writes them for readers); a mismatch with the gate prints `rigour_notice`. plan-phase and execute-phase print as their first line `Phase {N}: rigour {rigour} ({rigour_source}), risk domains: {names | none}, budget {wall} min / {dispatches} dispatches`, plus `rigour_rejected_reason` when the source is `rejected` and `rigour_notice` when set. Changing the spike phases of an already approved roadmap gate needs a new approval: repeat the request with `--revise`; the gate goes back to pending and blocks the roadmap until the owner approves it.

**Risk-domain add-ons** engage when the init field `risk_addons` is true, the one boolean every workflow and agent keys on: a `standard` phase whose `**Risk domains:**` line is not `none` (`phase_risk_domains`) or that has a plan with `high_risk: true` in its frontmatter (plan-phase also engages them when `quality assess-risk` newly marks a plan `high_risk`). The line is mandatory in every ROADMAP phase section: a comma list of single tokens from the project's `risk_domains`, or `none`; trailing prose is ignored, unknown tokens print a notice, and `owner-gate request --kind roadmap` fails when a phase that is not complete lacks it (the message names the phases and the exact line to add); a complete phase without it is one notice. For an old roadmap without the line `init plan-phase` matches the configured names against the phase goal and requirements and prints a notice.

## What runs

| | `spike` | `standard` | add-ons on a risk domain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purpose | throwaway feasibility: evidence, never product code | product work | product work on `risk_domains` / `ruling_topics` paths |
| Discuss / research | no discuss step; at most one research agent, and only when the phase has no `*-RESEARCH.md` | discuss when there is no CONTEXT; at most one research agent | same |
| Plans / tasks / lines per plan | 2 / 4 / 150 | 3 / 6 / 250 | same caps |
| Planner | `ruling_model` | `ruling_model` | `ruling_model` |
| Plan-check | 1 round, `checker_model`, Dimensions 1-7, 12, 15, 16 (traceability, every rigour); one revision, no re-check | cap 2 rounds, `checker_model`: round 1 full, round 2 delta | round 1 on `ruling_model` with the adversarial pass and `{plan}-VERDICT.md`; same cap |
| After the cap | proceed; leftovers listed | warnings become `A-` rows (`cheap`); remaining blockers get ONE planner revision and ONE targeted re-check of only those blockers (same checker tier, delta scope) | same, then a blocker that survives goes to ONE ruling on `ruling_model` before execution, never an OVERRIDE line alone |
| Tests | none: one capture file per question | tdd pairing, test-writer | + T0: real-system test with one named mutant per behaviour, rows in SUMMARY `## Test Delta` |
| Extra artifacts | `SPIKE-RESULT.md` (last spike phase) | none | ONE `{N}-THREAT-MODEL.md` per phase (abuse case + control, written by the planner before execution) |
| Implementation review | none: merged into the final review below (no separate reviewer dispatch, no review file) | cap 2 rounds, `reviewer_model`; round 2 reads only the fix commits; a CRITICAL/HIGH still open after the cap is fixed and re-checked by ONE targeted re-review of that finding (fresh reviewer, same tier, only its lines) or goes to ONE ruling, never an `A-` row | loop to zero findings, at most 4 rounds, then ONE ruling closes it; then ONE batched diff review on `ruling_model` |
| Final phase review (verifier, `ruling_model`) | the only review: no secret in a committed file or capture, every written path inside the spike roots or `.planning/`, each capture matches its command and is newer than the code it ran, every question answered yes/no/inconclusive. A disproved hypothesis is `passed`. Its `{N}-VERIFICATION.md` is the review record | product truths, wiring, test run, gates | + server-side authz read from the code, against the threat model |
| Ruling triggers | an effect that is irreversible outside the spike roots; an open CRITICAL/HIGH after the fix dispatch | irreversible, or legally or financially material | + a design choice, an accepted plan deviation or a loosened assertion on the risk-domain path |
| Gap closure / findings of the final review | ONE fix dispatch (`executor_model`) for the final review's findings: its brief carries every numbered fix and bounded probe of the review verbatim and its report gives each a status (done with capture path, or not done with reason); after it the coordinator re-runs the gate and spawns ONE fix re-check dispatch on `reviewer_model` that reads only the fix commits against the findings and the bounded probes' captures and reports per finding closed / still open; the coordinator lowers `open_critical_high` only for findings the re-check closed, recording `fixed_by` (fix commit sha, re-run rc) next to each; what stays open needs ONE ruling dispatch on `ruling_model` (allowed at `breach` like the final review, its fix and re-check, counted in the dispatch budget) that records its `ruled_by` id. Each condition a ruling imposes names the file that must carry it, and the coordinator checks the text landed there before recording `ruled_by`. The phase coordinator spawns it, inside the phase before it returns, and logs a `spike_ruling` event when it spawns it; if it returns without, `roadmap record` answers `ruling` and the roadmap orchestrator only re-enters the phase coordinator for it. A ruling counts only after the phase's latest `phase_start`, so a retried phase gets its own. Never silently parked; a non-CRITICAL/HIGH finding that was not mechanical stays listed as open in `SPIKE-RESULT.md` and the VERIFICATION body. A question still open is `inconclusive` | one cycle (light path for at most 4 small fixes); re-verify only what failed | one full cycle; re-verify only what failed |
| Budget, wall minutes | 45 min | 90 min | 150 min for the phase |

**Spike executor.** At spike the executor is the investigator, so `executor_model` (the floor, and with it the fix dispatch) resolves to `sonnet` (`executorModelFor` in `bin/gsd-tools.js`), and a plan runs as ONE executor on the highest tier tag among its tasks, never below that floor (`executor_model` per plan in `phase-plan-index`). Standard rigour runs one executor per task on the task's tag (`references/task-routing.md`).

**Retried parked spike phase.** A retry after a park is a new budget window (and "ONE fix dispatch" is per window); `budget status` and `phase_complete` also carry `total_wall_minutes` and `total_dispatches` across windows (metrics). On `--retry-parked` the phase coordinator resumes at `verify`: one fix dispatch carrying every still-open fix and bounded probe of the existing final review, its re-check, then the one ruling for what stays open; the top-model verifier is not re-run unless the ruling orders it.

**Spike path.** planner, checker, (one planner revision), executor(s), final review, (fix, fix re-check, ruling): the round caps above bound the dispatches, the wall budget bounds the cost; dispatches are counted, never limited. The `verify phase-gate` checks `test`, `docs`, `ui_qa` and `e2e_plan` are `not_applicable` at spike (the script resolves the rigour, so no `A-` waiver row and no `deferred add` is written for them); the review-file gate does not require a `{N}-REVIEW-{round}.md` at spike; `verify form --plans-only` skips `evidence_only_plan` at spike (a decision-record or evidence phase is legitimate there).

Not produced unless a cell says so: an E2E plan without UI, a threat model, mutants, a per-plan copy of a phase document, an evidence-only plan, an index or registry, findings copied between files.

## Rules for every phase

1. **Form is a script; the final review judges product truth.** Plan-time: the planner runs `gsd-tools verify form {N} --plans-only` (plan lines/tasks/plan-count caps over UNEXECUTED plans, evidence-only plan, missing `plan_stamp`) and fixes every error before plan-check; executed plans (with a SUMMARY) are never checked for caps. After execution `gsd-tools verify phase-gate {N} --pre-verifier` returns `failures` (missing WORK, including `missing_evidence`), `work_errors` and `form_errors` (SUMMARY Self-Check section missing; a planning-record line cite in a PLAN or SUMMARY the phase wrote; ledger rows are never checked). Form is `form_errors` only; never use `evidence_errors` or `work_errors` to decide form. Form errors go to one `executor_model` fix dispatch and a re-run, at most twice (never plan caps). A survivor is closed with `gsd-tools phase complete {N} --form-debt` (recorded in VERIFICATION frontmatter `form_debt`); it still refuses on missing work. A form debt never parks, never becomes `gaps_found`, never blocks dependents, and is never ruled on. A form issue the verifier notices goes in its report body and changes no status.
2. **Ids, not lines, for planning records.** Cite `D-34`, `A-25`, a requirement id, `Phase 2`, plan `02-01`, a task number. `DECISIONS.md:<line>` and `ROADMAP.md:<line>` are never written (a render moves them); to quote a record, quote its text. Code cites stay `file:line`.
3. **Evidence is a capture file**: the command and its output under `{phase_dir}/captures/`, written by the task that built the behaviour. SUMMARY names the path and restates no counts or hashes, so a fix round has nothing to re-reconcile.
4. **Plan-check converges.** Round 2 receives the round-1 issue list and `git diff` of the plans and answers two things: is each earlier finding closed, did a changed line break something. A new finding on unchanged text is a warning.
5. **Rulings only on the trigger row.** Anything else (a count, a literal, a name, a cleanup choice, wording) takes the stated default and one `A-` row logged `cheap`, with no dispatch. A ruling is one dispatch for all open items of a step.
6. **Plan size is checked at plan time, before the checker**, by `verify form {N} --plans-only`: every task counts; oversize goes back to the planner to split. Work that needs more plans than the cap is a phase that is too big: the planner says so in its return.
7. **Budget.** Defaults are the last table row; `workflow.budget.<spike|standard|risk>.wall_minutes` in config overrides (init field `budget_wall_minutes`). Dispatches are counted (`dispatches_spent`) but never limited: the round caps bound them and `warn`/`breach` use wall minutes alone. The coordinator calls `gsd-tools budget spend --phase {N}` once when it spawns any child agent (one dispatch) and `gsd-tools budget spend --phase {N} --done` when that child returns (extends the wall window, not a dispatch: a long running dispatch becomes visible), and `gsd-tools budget status --phase {N}` (`wall_minutes_spent`, `dispatches_spent`, `pct`, `warn`, `breach`) before each NEW cycle (plan-check round, review round, gap cycle). Wall time is the sum of work windows (phase start, or the first spend event when the phase has none, to the next record, park or complete, measured to the phase's last logged event, never the clock); idle time between sessions never counts. **At `warn` (80 %):** finish the step in progress and start no optional work (no extra review round, re-check, light-path gap cycle or second research). Mandatory steps still run: the final review and `phase complete`.
   **At `breach` (100 %):** start no new cycle (plan-check round, review round, gap cycle, retry, unstarted plan) and let the running child finish. When every plan has a SUMMARY the final review still runs ONCE, on `ruling_model`, and so does its one mandatory fix dispatch when it has a CRITICAL/HIGH finding (rule 11), and the one ruling for what the fix left open: none is ever skipped for budget; the breach stops everything after them. Then: the final review ran, `{N}-VERIFICATION.md` is `passed` and nothing mandatory was skipped: the phase completes and the breach is recorded in its `phase_complete` event; anything else: print an owner report of at most 15 lines naming each skipped step and each open finding and return `status: blocked`, `reason: budget_exceeded`.
   **Never silent.** A mandatory independent check that did not run (the final review; at `standard` and above also the implementation review) means the phase is not complete: `phase complete` refuses without a passed `{N}-VERIFICATION.md` (and, above spike, a `{N}-REVIEW-{round}.md`), and the coordinator never reports `completed` over a skipped check.
   When `phase complete` is recorded (`roadmap record`), its `phase_complete` event carries `wall_minutes_spent`, `dispatches_spent`, the budget and `breach`; `budget status --phase {N}` on a completed phase returns those recorded numbers (`final: true`).

8. **Top model.** `ruling_model` and `ruling_model_fallback` are the configured top model; `references/models.md` says where they come from and who always uses them. No other file names a model for those roles.
9. **Plan stamp.** The planner copies `plan_stamp` from an `init plan-phase` call made immediately before the planner spawn into each PLAN frontmatter (hash of the ROADMAP phase section, the phase's requirement lines, CONTEXT and RESEARCH; no ledger rows: what a plan depends on is in CONTEXT). `plans_stale: true` is consumed once, in plan-phase.md; never re-plan on a guess.

10. **The review is a disk check.** `{N}-REVIEW-{round}.md` carries frontmatter `round`, `reviewer_role`, `open_critical_high` (count still open after the round) and, after a ruling, `ruled_by: [D-NN]`. `verify phase-gate` and `phase complete` report missing work when a phase that changed files outside `.planning/` (a spike is exempt: its VERIFICATION file is the review record) has no review file or its newest round has open CRITICAL/HIGH and no ruling id (`bin/review-gate.js`).

11. **Question traceability, negative answers, final-review questions (every rigour; stated once here, role files point to it).**
   - **Traceability (plan time).** The planner lists, per question / success criterion, its source requirement AND the source PRD's exit or acceptance criteria it comes from (path + heading or id), and every source criterion the plan does NOT cover with a reason. The plan-checker's single round opens the PRD and verifies that list; an uncovered criterion without a stated out-of-scope reason is a blocker.
   - **Negative answers (spike).** An answer "no / does not work / none exists" needs (a) a root cause shown in a capture, or (b) a recorded search of the installed package or source for the error text and for sibling options (command and output in a capture). Otherwise the answer is `inconclusive`, and an `inconclusive` row needs the same (a) or (b), run in the phase that can still act on what it finds: the search reads the failure text and sibling options (adapters, modes, flags) in the installed package and docs. The final review downgrades any "no" lacking (a)/(b). The script check: the answer table (below) names that capture for every row but `yes`; `verify phase-gate` reports a WORK error for a row without one.
   - **Final-review questions (top model).** For every conclusion or recommendation: (i) does each reason against the rejected option apply ONLY to it, or equally to the chosen one; (ii) could the probe or test have produced the opposite result (a probe that cannot fail, an assertion loosened to pass, a status-only check); (iii) are conditions joined correctly (and / or); (iv) was the chosen option proven to the same standard as the rejected one; also what did an install or probe change in shared state that a product would inherit; (v) at spike, how many of the phase's exit criteria (traced at plan time) have a decided pass or fail: the verifier writes `criteria_decided: <n>/<m>`, and with n = 0 the result headline is "undecided: next probes ..." (frontmatter `headline: undecided`), with no recommendation or default; a recommendation cites the decided criteria it rests on. Findings are fixed before the phase is complete: ONE fix dispatch is mandatory when the review has any CRITICAL/HIGH finding. The review writes frontmatter `open_critical_high` and, after a ruling, `ruled_by: [D-NN]` into `{N}-VERIFICATION.md`; `verify phase-gate` and `phase complete` refuse while it lists an open CRITICAL/HIGH with no ruling id (`bin/review-gate.js`, `verificationReviewErrors`).

## Spike answer table

`SPIKE-RESULT.md` (or the VERIFICATION body) holds one table per phase, one row per question; it is the only per-phase result record at spike:

```
| Question | Answer | Capture | Probe | Root cause or search |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 does the admin answer on workerd | inconclusive | `captures/q1.txt` | `spike/probes/q1.sh` | |
| Q2 is there a database adapter | no | `captures/q2.txt` | | `captures/q2-search.txt` |
```

Answer is `yes`, `no` or `inconclusive`. `bin/spike-check.js` (called by `verify phase-gate` and `phase complete` at spike) reports a WORK error for a row other than `yes` whose Root cause or search cell is empty or names a file that is not on disk, and for a stale capture: the Probe script is newer than its Capture (last commit time, or mtime when dirty or untracked). A form error is reported when ROADMAP marks the phase complete while REQUIREMENTS still shows one of its requirements pending or STATE still calls it in progress. At spike a plan SUMMARY is at most 15 lines (plan id, commits, capture paths, Self-Check): it restates no result, count or conclusion. No `index.tsv` or registry is written, and no `DEFERRED.json` entry for a `not_applicable` gate.

## Never relaxed

1. No secret in a committed file, capture or command output; captures are scanned.
2. No push, deploy or remote mutation by a spike beyond a throwaway target named in its authorisation. Hooks and guardrails are untouched.
3. No real user data and no production credentials in a spike.
4. A spike writes nothing outside its roots and `.planning/`.
5. Spike output is never promoted: no `standard` plan lists a spike-root path in `files_modified`, imports from it or cites spike evidence as a test REUSE.
6. One executor at a time per phase; owner gates OG1-OG3; the decisions ledger records every assumption.

## Guards

- **G1 fail closed.** `spike` without the newest approved roadmap gate listing the phase in `--spike-phases` with `--spike-roots`, or without roots, resolves to `standard` with `rigour_source: rejected`. No `D-` or `A-` row authorises a spike. Any other value on the Rigour line, including the retired `high`, resolves to `standard` with a visible notice.
- **G2 containment.** A spike plan `files_modified` entry outside the roots and `.planning/` is a `rigour_violation`; plan-phase sends it back to the planner ONCE (offending paths and the gate's `spike_roots` named), re-runs the check, and a second violation stops plan-phase before the checker. The planner always plans against the GATE's roots (init `spike_roots`); a ROADMAP `**Spike roots**` line that differs is informational.
- **G3 production disjoint.** A spike root may not equal or contain a `stack.style.roots` or `stack.paths` entry.
- **G4 no relabelling.** A phase that already has executed plans (a SUMMARY) cannot be newly added to `--spike-phases`; phases the approved gate already lists are not re-checked by a `--revise`.
- **G5 audit.** The milestone audit lists every spike phase and confirms by `git diff --stat` over its commits that G2 held.

## Promotion

The last spike phase writes `SPIKE-RESULT.md`: per question the answer, the capture path, go/no-go, what must be rebuilt. ONE batched ruling (owner for business calls) turns it into `D-` rows. `complete-milestone` records spike questions as answered, not as validated requirements. The product milestone is planned from scratch at `standard`; spike roots are read-only reference.
